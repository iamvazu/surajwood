## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Royale Touche dominates organic search in the Indian surfaces/interiors market through three core strategies that SurajWood currently lacks:

1. **Massive keyword-rich content on the homepage** — 3,000+ words of SEO copy targeting 100+ keyword variations (laminates, sunmica, acrylic, etc.)
2. **Programmatic SEO (pSEO) via Design Ideas hub** — 60+ individual pages targeting room-by-room and sub-category design keywords (e.g., "L shape kitchen design", "wardrobe design for small bedroom", "pooja room design")
3. **Color-based category pages** — Dedicated landing pages for every color (green laminates, black laminates, pink laminates, etc.)

SurajWood has a technically cleaner, modern Next.js site with good meta tags and some GEO pages — but is fundamentally under-indexed with only ~20 total pages vs Royale Touche's 200+ pages.
